My approach

Architecture starts with the right questions.

  1. 01

    Clarify requirements

    Define availability, performance, security, recovery, and budget needs.

  2. 02

    Design for failure

    Isolate components and remove avoidable single points of failure.

  3. 03

    Secure every layer

    Consider identity, network, compute, data, and operational access.

  4. 04

    Make decisions visible

    Document assumptions, trade-offs, and validation steps.

  5. 05

    Improve iteratively

    Review the result against the AWS Well-Architected pillars.
